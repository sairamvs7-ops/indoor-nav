# Via launch film: engagement hooks (final cut, 2:02)

These are intentional open questions, put in so the Dean asks the team about the future. On screen they are only questions or "next" teasers, never claims. The prepared answers are in `PROJECT_VIDEO_BRIEF.md` §17.

## In the film

| Hook | Where | On screen | Left open | Honest boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D. Accuracy without beacons** | 0:25, start of the Scan scene | "No beacons. No Wi-Fi. **How does it know?**" | *How accurate* (no numbers given) | The film shows the mechanism: exact at a marker; walk mode is labelled BETA, "position between markers is estimated" |
| **G. Accessibility** | 0:38, preferences | "**Can everyone find their way?**" + real Step-free / Avoid lifts | Readiness for screen readers and audits | Only the routing preferences (Step-free, Avoid lifts) are shown working |
| **C. Campus-wide rollout** | 1:35, Future | "**One block today. Every block next.**" + campus plan + PLANNED "One shared map library" | When, how many blocks, who maps them, cost | Only one block (the sample) is set up today; the campus plan is an illustration |
| **I. Other applications** | 1:41, Beyond campus | "Anywhere people get lost indoors." Hospitals, Malls, Airports, Exam halls (narrated) | Which market first; any validation | Spoken as "can help"; no deployments claimed |
| **E. Future development** | 1:45 | "What if you could walk through a building just once?" → PLANNED → "**Next: Walk it once. It maps itself.**" (now narrated: "one day…") | Timeline, method, funding | Not implemented; it has a PLANNED label |
| Closing line | 1:57 | "Built for our college · Via" | The ask: a pilot on our own campus | — |

## Rules for re-editing
1. A hook is a question or a "Next" teaser. It is never an answer, a statistic or a price.
2. Nothing planned appears without the PLANNED pill, and nothing beta without BETA.
3. The only numbers in the film are 36/756, 784/784 and 192. All three are verified from the code (see `NARRATION_SCRIPT.md`).
